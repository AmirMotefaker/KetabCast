# v0.2.0-beta.5.1.19 — Multi-Generation Segment Provenance Re-anchor

## Trigger

The first controlled production run from v0.2.0-beta.5.1.18 failed during resume seeding before any TTS generation.

- Production run: 32134056465
- Exact source: v0.2.0-beta.5.1.18 / 99e160a1919a8b5ab074e5e38d06a41bb159c05c
- Failed artifact: 9323290268
- Failed artifact digest: sha256:cde3ed6b936a39118438e5ff10bda53c61842de361d64b993205afcb0037409a
- Failure: Segment checkpoint mismatch: atomic-habits/male/chunks/01-segments/001.wav
- The failure occurred in dual-voice-resume-seed.mjs before the resume-seed PASS marker and before any Gemini TTS request.
- The r16 failed artifact is not used as the next resume source.

## Root cause

The valid Schedar segment checkpoints in the last complete checkpoint artifact were generated in r14 / v0.2.0-beta.5.1.16 and safely reused in r15 / v0.2.0-beta.5.1.17.

Their sidecars still retained the original sourceCodeSha/sourceRunId. The v0.2.0-beta.5.1.18 resume seed incorrectly required every segment sidecar to match the direct resume run/source exactly, even when all content identity, WAV hash, duration, and ancestry remained valid.

## Released contract

- Keep transcript wording, segment plan <=180 words, 0.25 second/word coverage floor, and v0.2.0-beta.5.1.18 bounded coverage recovery unchanged.
- Segment checkpoint and segment pending sidecars may cross one or more resume generations only when:
  - sourceCodeSha and sourceRunId are valid;
  - sourceRunId is not newer than the direct resume source run;
  - the prior source SHA is an ancestor/equal of the direct resume source SHA;
  - book/role/voice/chunk/segment/path/words/script/chunk/segment hashes remain exact;
  - checkpoint WAV hash and duration remain exact and coverage-valid.
- On successful seed, copied segment checkpoint/pending sidecars are re-anchored to the direct resume source/run and retain their previous source in reusedFrom.
- When the renderer reuses a segment checkpoint in a production run, it rewrites that checkpoint sidecar to the current GITHUB_SHA/GITHUB_RUN_ID and retains previous provenance in reusedFrom.
- No blind provenance relaxation: unrelated, malformed, or future-run provenance remains rejected.
- Pending Interaction same-ID semantics, SSE streaming, hardNetworkCap=24, 12-second pacing, quota handling, content_blocked fail-closed, and MAX_SEGMENT_AUDIO_COVERAGE_ATTEMPTS=2 remain unchanged.
